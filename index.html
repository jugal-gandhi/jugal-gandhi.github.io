Research

My research interests lie at the intersection of hardware security, VLSI design, and cryptographic hardware. My research intrest includes developing hardware security techniques and architectures for trustworthy IC design. My work studies both the attack side and the defense side of this problem.

Research and development experience

Project Associate-IIAug 2026 – Present
VLSI Design Lab, CSIR-Central Electronics Engineering Research Institute (CEERI), Pilani
Senior Research Fellow (GATE)Oct 2022 – Sept 2025
CSIR-CEERI, Pilani. Sponsored by CSIR-HRDG, Government of India
  • Developed KAVACH, a CAD framework for zero-knowledge logic obfuscation. It automates security-primitive integration and adversarial evaluation on benchmark netlists and IPs.
  • Designed and demonstrated ULTRON, a logic locking attack with 100% key recovery on SARLock- and IOLTS-protected netlists within seconds.
  • Proposed DyLock, a dynamic key-based logic locking technique with below 6% overhead. It resists Boolean satisfiability and structural attacks on standard benchmarks.
  • Developed a resource-efficient modulo reduction unit for the number theoretic transform (NTT) in Dilithium, with improved area and logic utilization.
Junior Research Fellow (GATE)Sept 2020 – Sept 2022
CSIR-CEERI, Pilani. Sponsored by CSIR-HRDG, Government of India
  • Proposed the gated MUX cascade-based SHAKTI and SHAKTI+ locking schemes, with design overheads below 20% on benchmark netlists.
  • Demonstrated a key-aliasing effect for SHAKTI in power trace analysis.
  • Proposed LOKI, a lightweight key-controlled locking technique for IoT IPs with 2–5% FPGA resource overhead, validated on the GIFT cipher.
  • Performed a cryptanalytic evaluation of lightweight 4-bit S-boxes.
